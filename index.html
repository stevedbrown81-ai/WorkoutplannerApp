01

Eight areas every session

Chest, back, shoulders, quads, hamstrings, calves, biceps and triceps.

02

Rotate movement patterns

Balance rows and pulldowns, curls and hinges, flat and incline pressing—without making training random.

03

Focus lifts first

The most demanding, technical or important movements lead. Isolation work comes later.

04

Two quality sets

A modest volume of repeatable work. Most working sets finish around RPE 7–9.

05

Progress like for like

Compare each exercise with its own previous performance, not with another movement for the same area.

06

Recovery changes the choice

Reduce load or use stable movements when fatigue is high. Do not cram missed sessions together.
