The rulesHow workouts are built

Most strength movements use two quality working sets. Exercise choice follows your requested session, location, energy and pain—not a rigid weekly split.

  • Four to seven familiar, useful exercises
  • Stable and joint-friendly choices when readiness is lower
  • Home sessions use your kettlebells, rings, bands, parallettes, pull-up bar and mace
  • Kongs sessions prioritise the preferred machine, cable and dumbbell library
