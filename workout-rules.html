Workout rules.

The concise rules enforced by the offline selection engine.

01

The requested session wins

The planner adapts exercise stability and range, not the body part requested. Pain prompts safe substitutions or a clearly explained omission; it does not silently turn strength work into Mobility.

02

Two quality working sets

Upper-body compounds normally use 6–12 reps. Isolation work uses higher reps. Loaded leg work uses 10–20 reps to keep loading sensible; athletic kettlebell complexes may use short repeated sequences. Rest and RIR are left to Steve.

03

Time changes structure

Twenty minutes is a lighter two-round pump circuit. Thirty minutes or less uses efficient supersets. Forty to fifty minutes is normal coverage. Sixty minutes adds every useful accessory. Warm-up takes 5–7 minutes inside that time.

04

Session contracts

  • Push: chest, shoulder press and triceps; lateral delts when time allows.
  • Pull: vertical pull, horizontal row, biceps and rear-delt/shoulder health.
  • Legs: squat, unilateral/lunge, direct quads and hamstrings.
  • Upper: chest press, shoulder press, vertical pull and row; arms and rear delts over 30 minutes.
  • Full Body: lower/lunge, both presses, both pulls and arms.
05

Permanent joint-friendly constraints

No barbell pressing, straight-bar curls, underhand pulldowns or carries. Neutral-grip pulling and curls are preferred. Loaded squat and lunge depth stops at the clinician-advised 45° limit; bodyweight lower work uses a comfortable unweighted range.

06

Location is a hard constraint

Kongs favours familiar machines, cables and dumbbells. Home uses known kettlebells, rings, bands and parallettes; neutral-grip pull-ups use the rings or outdoor climbing frame, while the pull-up bar uses a standard grip. Hotel uses only selected equipment. No Equipment assumes no bar or anchor and omits pulling instead of inventing weak substitutes.

07

Readiness stays simple

Low keeps complete pattern coverage using controlled, mentally easier choices. Medium is the normal default. High favours harder primary choices without relaxing permanent restrictions. Readiness resets daily.

08

Variation follows completed work

Only completed workouts enter history. Familiar primary variations rotate across comparable sessions. Reviewed Favourite and Like movements receive a gentle bias, not a lock, so approved variety remains visible. Swaps are one-off and history records what was actually performed.

09

Special sessions

Bodyweight is full body. Home Kettlebell selects one complete, familiar single- or double-kettlebell session and rotates the whole workout. Armour Building is the favourite recurring anchor; Simple & Sinister, single-bell, double-bell and simplified Warrior formats provide variation. Deck of Cards and technical chains are excluded from normal selection. Specific pain selections use the safer substitution engine. Carries stay out.

10

The weekly guide

All seven days can be assigned a workout or Rest. Full sessions are generated in advance and copied cleanly to Apple Notes. The plan stays fixed; if circumstances change, use the daily generator.
